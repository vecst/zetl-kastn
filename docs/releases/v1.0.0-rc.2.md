# Zetl 1.0.0 RC 2

The second release candidate. The headline: Zetl now teaches itself. A short
tour opens the first time you run it, measures how long your own taps and
holds take, and has you try each hold for real.

Download `Zetl-v1.0.0-rc.2-win-x64.zip`, extract it, and run `Zetl.exe`. It's
not yet code-signed, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning: choose **More
info → Run anyway**. Your notes and settings from RC 1 carry over.

## New

- **The tour.** It measures your taps and holds and sets the hold timing to fit
  your hands, then walks through a quick note, a capture, a cut taken back with
  `Esc`, the Board, and Compose. It's in the tray menu as **Take the Tour**.
- **Measure My Taps and Holds** in the tray menu runs the measuring on its own,
  any time, and suggests a hold timing.
- **A hold indicator.** A small ring in the top right fills while you hold.
  **Detailed** (the default) also shows the keys and how long you held them;
  **Ring only** and **Off** are in Settings → Hold Actions.
- **Settings → Hold Actions** lists every tap and hold and lets you change what
  each one does, with separate choices for file lists and for Zetl's own
  windows.
- **Popup position and transparency.** Popups can open top centre, centred, in
  a corner, or at the mouse pointer, and their opacity is on the Theme page.

## Changed

- **Compile is now Compose.** A held `Ctrl+V` opens it.
  - **Where it starts:** on your active project, or, with none active, on the
    project you last added to.
  - **`Ctrl+Enter`:** pastes straight into the app you came from. A setting
    makes it copy instead.
  - **TSV:** just the table, with no project or bucket names, and it pastes
    straight into cells in Excel and LibreOffice Calc with no import dialog.
  - **Formatted:** a checkbox turns the project and bucket headings off.
- **Consumable templates hand back control.** When one runs out, Zetl returns
  to the project you were on, and the template picker opens on the template
  you last used.
- **Activating a project is always your choice.** Saving a capture never
  switches projects behind your back. A new setting can save every copy to
  your Journal when no project is active.
- **Inside Zetl and Kastn, shortcuts behave normally.**
  - A tapped `Ctrl+V` while editing a slip is an ordinary paste, never Replay.
  - A copy isn't saved as a duplicate slip.
  - Holds inside a Zetl popup don't open another popup.
- **Pastes into File Explorer and the desktop** go straight through, even with
  Replay or Pop on.
- **Faster capture.** Zetl reads less on every `Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+X`, so holds
  feel quicker.
- **Dialogs open fully drawn**, without a flash of empty glass.

## Fixed

- **Paste could stop working** after cutting files in File Explorer. Zetl
  could refuse to touch a clipboard it couldn't read and swallow the paste.
  Pastes now always go through.
- **Private copies.** Copies that a password manager marks as private are no
  longer read or saved.
- **Journal bucket choice.** A bucket you picked in the Journal is no longer
  overridden by the day's default.
- **Reserved names.** Renaming a bucket to `Scratch` or `Deleted` could hide
  its slips. Those names are now reserved.
- **Kastn crash** on a picture it couldn't decode.
- **Clipboard backups on managed work machines.** Zetl no longer calls an
  undocumented Windows function, which could corrupt memory.

## Known issues

- On rare occasions Zetl has closed unexpectedly during very fast, repeated
  `Ctrl+C` presses on the desktop. If it happens to you, please
  [open an issue](https://github.com/vecst/zetl-kastn/issues) and attach
  `%AppData%\Zetl\diagnostics.log`.
